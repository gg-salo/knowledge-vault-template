# /reading/playbooks/

External playbooks captured from the reading pile. The mirror of `/me/playbooks/` (your authored playbooks, empirical) — this directory holds playbooks gobbled from outside: community libraries, expert posts, other vaults, books.

## What lives here

Long-form, multi-module operational frameworks authored by others. Each file follows the template at `/me/playbooks/_TEMPLATE.md` with:
- `source: external`
- `source_url` filled in
- `evidence_level: theoretical` until validated in your own work
- `scope: [external]` unless explicitly federated

## What does NOT live here

- Short skills → `/skills/inbox/`
- Articles, repos, threads → `/reading/articles/`, `/reading/repos/`, `/reading/threads/`
- External doctrines (named principles) → `/reading/doctrines/`
- Your own playbooks → `/me/playbooks/`
